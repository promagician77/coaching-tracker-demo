How it fits together

  1. Client checks inA simple form on their phone, or straight into their sheet if they prefer.
  2. Their private sheet updatesEach client has their own copy, so no one sees anyone else's numbers.
  3. Your dashboard pulls from everyoneOne screen, colored by status, sorted by who needs you most.

Why one sheet per client

In Google Sheets, protecting a tab only stops edits. It doesn't hide the data. Put every client in one shared file and each of them can see the others' weight and notes. Separate sheets keep everyone private, and your dashboard still sees them all.

Adding a client takes one click

A "Coaching" menu in your dashboard creates a new client sheet from the template, shares it with their email, and adds them to your dashboard.
